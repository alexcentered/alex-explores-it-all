    What you are looking at

    Why there are only five

    A regular solid has the same regular polygon for every face and the same number of them at every corner. At a corner, the angles that meet must add up to less than a full turn, 360°. Otherwise the faces lie flat, or cannot close at all. Only five combinations pass:

    Euclid ended his Elements, about 300 BC, with the proof that there are no others.

    Duals

    Put a corner at the middle of each face of a solid and join them up: you get its dual. The cube and octahedron are each other's duals, as are the dodecahedron and icosahedron. The tetrahedron's dual is another tetrahedron.

    Solids and waves

    A sphere has standing waves, as a plate does. Each has a degree, and for most degrees the wave can be arranged with the full symmetry of a Platonic solid. This page shows the lowest that can for each: degree 3, 4 and 6. Watching one swing shows what a dual is. At one end of the swing the surface bulges at the corners of a solid; at the other end, at the corners of its dual. A cube and an octahedron are the two halves of one vibration.

    This is why the same few symmetries turn up in crystals, viruses, drops and molecules. They are the arrangements that are possible for a round thing, whatever it is made of.

    Fractals

    A fractal is made of smaller copies of itself. If a shape is made of N copies each s times smaller, its dimension is log N ÷ log s. For a line, square or cube that gives 1, 2 or 3. For a fractal it gives something in between. Coastlines, lungs, river networks and lightning are rough fractals over a range of sizes: they branch or wrinkle the same way at each scale. The sand lines on a plate are not; they are smooth curves.

    The spark

    The first two fractals are drawn by a rule. The spark is grown. It advances one short step at a time, more often where the electric field at its edge is stronger, and the field is worked out again after every step. Nothing in the rule mentions branching; the branches come from the way a sharp tip concentrates the field and so tends to grow, while the gaps between tips are shielded and tend not to. Lightning, the streamers from a Tesla coil and the figures a spark burns into plastic form this way. Grown from a point with the standard rule, the published dimension is about 1.7, and the page measures each one it grows.

    History, kept separate from physics

    Plato, in the Timaeus about 360 BC, gave four of the solids to the elements (fire, earth, air, water) and the fifth to the cosmos. That is philosophy, and it is shown here as his assignment and nothing more. In 1596 Kepler tried to explain the spacing of the planets by nesting the five solids inside each other. It does not fit the measurements, and he later found the laws that do.

    What is not here

    Only the lowest symmetric wave is shown for each solid; higher ones exist. The fractals stop after a few steps, because every step multiplies the number of pieces. The spark is a flat, idealised model: real lightning is three-dimensional, and its heating, stepped leaders and return strokes are left out.
